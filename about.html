Our Journey

BUILT FOR ATHLETES, DESIGNED FOR EVERYONE

Founded in 2015, Fista Fitness started with a clear vision: to create an empowering environment where individuals of all athletic backgrounds can push past physical limitations and achieve true body transformations.

Our 25,000 sq ft facility houses state-of-the-art powerlifting racks, extensive cardio decks, Olympic turf tracks, and dedicated group studios for HIIT, Yoga, and Boxing.
